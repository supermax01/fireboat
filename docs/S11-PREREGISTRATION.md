# S11 prospective measurement protocol, revision 1

Status: **preregistered tooling; corpus generation, development runs and heldout measurement unrun**. No efficiency claim or default change is established. This document and `scripts/s11/study.ts` define the prospective study. Root must review and freeze their exact source hashes before executing the driver. The driver records those hashes before generation, freezes the selected corpus and queries before development, and freezes development observations before the first heldout task. No heldout chart, capture, patient identity or local run manifest belongs in git or the npm package.

Contract: [S8–S9 design §6](../proposals/m3b-s8s9-design.md), [M3b §4 and Addendum C](../proposals/m3b-spec.md), [release plan S10–S11](../proposals/release-plan.md), the cited GPT acceptance and P6, and [compact profile status](COMPACT-PROFILES.md). Addendum C supersedes the old 0.50 internal target. S11 does not close S10, real-host verification, autonomous model success, clinical, SMART, vendor or competitor gates.

## Corpus and selection, frozen before screening

Thirty natural Synthea charts: 10 development (3 small, 3 medium, 4 large) and 20 heldout (6 small, 6 medium, 8 large), disjoint by patient and generator seed. Count the complete living patient's transaction resources and their outward reference closure. Shared statewide hospital/practitioner exports count only where actually referenced. Small = 1–99 resources; medium = 100–999; large = 1,000–30,000. The 30,000-resource upper bound is a prospective computational cap; exceeding it is an explicit exclusion, never a reason to extend the seed pool. Count the natural chart before the lab-view transform. The private raw exports retain the full chart; the measurement view is its reference-closed in-window laboratory graph.

| Split | Small, ages 0–5 | Medium, ages 20–40 | Large, ages 60–85 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Development | 100000–100127; take first 3 eligible | 101000–101127; take first 3 eligible | 102000–102127; take first 4 eligible |
| Heldout | 200000–200127; take first 6 eligible | 201000–201127; take first 6 eligible | 202000–202127; take first 8 eligible |

Seed order is increasing, with no gaps. Each seed generates one living patient; clinician seed equals patient seed. Each stratum stops at its quota or its 128th seed. An eligible large chart also has **at least 300 eligible Observations and at least 131,072 bytes of sanitized upstream Observation evidence**. An eligible Observation belongs to that living patient, has the laboratory category coding `http://terminology.hl7.org/CodeSystem/observation-category|laboratory`, and its effective interval lies wholly in `[2020-01-01T00:00:00Z,2026-01-01T00:00:00Z)`, as implemented by the reviewed transform's UTC interval parser. There is no HbA1c, numeric-value, component, status or compact-outcome screening filter. Observation bytes are the sum of each qualifying resource's complete lossless compact-JSON sanitized FHIR serialization, UTF-8, without wrappers or line separators. `sanitize` is used here only for that upstream size predicate, never a compact projection or token measurement.

Screening records every attempted seed, chart count, eligible Observation count, sanitized Observation bytes and fixed exclusion code. Unavailable counts on refused charts are `null`, never fabricated zeroes. Screening sees no compact output or token outcome. A generation or repeatability failure aborts the study. A chart/reference/transform refusal is recorded as an exclusion. A depleted pool leaves the corpus **unfulfilled/unmeasured**; no efficiency run is allowed. No module tuning, seed extension, size-band revision, replacement patient, padding, duplicated administrative root, quickstart edge or augmentation is allowed. A later changed study needs a new prospective protocol and a valid holdout; changing the run name cannot make an already unsealed patient a new holdout.

## Tasks, arms, operating point and scope

The primary heldout denominator is **eight large patients × three templates = 24 paired instances**, clustered by patient. These are three clinical concepts, not 24 independent concepts. Small/medium heldout charts contribute inventories and split integrity; they do not enter the primary ratio. Run the same three templates on all 10 development charts (30 development instances). Per era and format, record all instances, including unsupported and capped attempts.

1. **Bounded multi-page lab trend:** discover Observation support and the date parameter, search the laboratory category over the fixed wide window with page size 20, and drain pages. Require the exact natural Observation identity set, with no duplicates, and complete response-bound lab facts and citations.
2. **Recent HbA1c evidence:** the same discovery, system-qualified LOINC `4548-4`, the same explicit window and requested `_sort=-date`, draining all results. No natural HbA1c is `unsupported/NO_NATURAL_HBA1C`, not a replacement seed or cheap success. Requested sorting remains unverified under the generic adapter profile; this task never asserts a proved latest result or complete source absence.
3. **Panel/component evidence with field recovery:** select the lowest type/id component Observation; if none, the lowest natural DiagnosticReport with at least two resolvable Observation results. Read it, inspect its retained handle, project its retained clinical fields plus encounter/note, and for a report read every member Observation. Check the associations, retained binding, facts and numeric spellings in their own responses. Neither form present is `unsupported/NO_NATURAL_PANEL`. There is no constructed panel and no omission of extra members to meet a budget.

All templates use actual `FhirGateway`, the MCP adapter and SDK client over the eval runner's instrumented in-memory transport. The backend is the existing synthetic fixture **adapter serving only this natural chart**, with no fixture corpus fragments, adversarial edges or injected metadata versions. This is source-gateway measurement, not HAPI, installed-package, real-host or autonomous-model performance. The source adapter identity, gateway revision, negotiated era and existing eval checks/work/byte accounting accompany every result. HAPI execution is unnecessary for this protocol and is not performed by the driver.

Run modern and legacy eras separately, with the existing format arms `fhir`, `compact` and `selected`. Pair `compact` with `fhir` only within the same patient, template, era, delivery profile, input bytes and operating point. `selected` is descriptive. All arms use the same discovery, recovery, page and inspection plan. Explicit selected fields are frozen in `tasks.ts`; concrete choice fields are supplied, never invalid `[x]` wire field names. Panel/report fields are added prospectively by template. Per-call limit/default = 24,576 B; configured maximum = 131,072 B; page size = 20; per-task ceiling = 16,777,216 result bytes, 256 tool calls, 128 upstream calls, and the existing runner's 50 automatic page-continuation cap. Zero harness retries. Every attempted error, page, local remainder, inspection, recovery and duplicate delivery counts. Transport-internal work remains independently counted by the existing eval observer. Ceilings are failure outcomes, never excluded observations.

The scripted full-text evaluation host delivers exactly the captured SDK text blocks joined by LF per tool result. `structuredContent` is unavailable at its model-input boundary. Save these actual message strings, requests, server-emitted serialized result values and hashes before offline tokenization. Check critical facts on both wire and captured text using the existing response-local `citedFact` oracle, including exact raw numeric tokens, full coding/text/quantity/reference-range/component values, meaning roots, source/id/version and valid indexed provenance. Source expectations come from the frozen natural view; a different response cannot repair a missing fact. Missing captures or critical facts fail the attempt. Retained inspection/projection checks reuse the existing oracle. Actual deployment-host receipt remains a separate unverified claim.

Only `full-text-v1` has an executable lane. The shipped registry has zero verified structured-delivery cells. Report structured/slim and same-format paired delivery savings as **unmeasured**; do not invent a host, assume model-visible structuredContent, or enable the private test override. A future verified host cell requires a new prospective measurement amendment before its holdout is unsealed.

## Frozen metric and report rules

The following three rules are the exact report summary rules:

> C.1: Target median paired total model-visible result tokens Tcompact/Tfhir ≤0.90, separately per era × eligible delivery profile × pinned tokenizer. Require 24 complete paired successes, 100% critical fact/citation/meaning checks, no deterministic task-success loss, and every applicable required fidelity mutant killed. A failed pair cannot count as an efficiency success.
>
> C.2: Report absolute model-visible bytes and tokens per correctly answered lab question, median/p90 and every attempted outcome, with complete wire-result and frame bytes separately. Include discovery, errors, recovery, pages, retained work and duplicates. No numerical delivery-reduction threshold is adopted; failed answers are failures, not cheap successes.
>
> C.3: Target median per-task (provenance + withheld-entry + coverage bytes) / structuredContent bytes ≤0.15, separately for fhir and compact across the same 24 complete primary tasks. Sum component values and complete structuredContent bytes over all result messages before dividing; then take the median over tasks. A miss is target-unmet independently of C.1/C.2.

C.1's point target and statistical-smaller requirement are distinct. Calculate the median of the 24 **per-task paired** token ratios and p90, not a ratio of aggregate bytes or aggregate tokens. Bootstrap eight patients carrying their three paired tasks together, with 10,000 resamples, xorshift32 shifts 13/17/5, unsigned state, seed `0x4d336231`, index `floor(8*state/2^32)`. Use Hyndman–Fan type 7 linear quantiles for the [2.5th,97.5th] percentile interval and all medians/p90s. Order clusters by frozen patient label and templates by the frozen list. “smaller” requires the 95% upper endpoint **<1.0**; “not_smaller” means lower endpoint **≥1.0**; otherwise “indeterminate.” Eight clusters limit precision.

Unfulfilled strata, fewer than 24 successful pairs, absent model captures, zero/invalid token denominators or missing reference tokenization make qualifying C.1 **unmeasured**. Report attempted outcomes and available descriptive statistics anyway. Exploratory macOS counts and their distributions are labelled exploratory, never qualifying reference-tokenizer acceptance. A missing exact-source fidelity-mutant aggregate leaves C.1 acceptance unmeasured even when the descriptive ratio is smaller. Freeze the required mutant IDs and official candidate binding before generation: all registered mutants affecting core projection/privacy/decimal/withheld/contracts/index/store, server adapter, eval runner/oracle, or S11 tooling. If supplied via `--mutation-shards`, the driver reads and purely validates all 14 official shard reports with the existing aggregator; it never executes the mutation judge. Each required ID must be killed in that valid exact-source aggregate. Missing evidence is not a pass.

For a complete reference-measured cell, publish the median, p90, interval and actual absolute distributions whether the result is positive, null or negative. A statistically smaller cell missing the ≤0.90 point target is **target-unmet**. A not-smaller or indeterminate result is reported under that literal label. No post-hoc exclusions, favorable tokenizer/profile choice, selective rerun or later format flip is allowed. FHIR/full-text defaults remain unchanged by this tooling; any later default proposal requires every advertised applicable cell to satisfy the C.1 target, significance, fidelity, task-success and mutation gates. Delivery savings cannot establish a C.1 win. All unsupported, unmeasured, capped and failed attempts remain visible.

C.2 success-only cost distributions explicitly give their successful-answer denominator beside all attempted failures/unsupported results and all-attempt wire/framing distributions. Zero-cost unsupported attempts never enter correct-answer costs. Report each format separately. Catalog, tool-call request, model-visible result, complete wire-result and combined task tokens are distinct columns. A catalog is charged once per task/arm from actual SDK discovery/listing, tool-call requests use compact `{name,arguments}` values, and combined task = catalog + requests + model-visible results. JSON-RPC/handshake metadata are protocol overhead, not hidden in or conflated with this combined semantic boundary. Result framing bytes remain separately recorded by the existing runner.

C.3's numerator is the disjoint serialized **values** of `provenance`, `transform.withheld_payloads` when present, and `coverage`. Include each value's object/array delimiters; exclude its enclosing key/separator. Absent values contribute zero. Preserve exact numeric spellings and UTF-8; do not reserialize decoded numeric values to count bytes. Denominators include the entire serialized `structuredContent`. Also report each component's absolute bytes, the remaining non-resource envelope bytes, all-task distributions and outcomes. A failed/incomplete primary cohort leaves qualifying C.3 unmeasured; a measured median above 0.15 is target-unmet. Neither a low share nor delivery reduction permits dropping required provenance, omissions or controls.

## Offline tokenizers and staged freezes

Reuse `measure-tool-catalog.py`'s offline loader, `scripts/requirements-tokenizers.txt`, and `packages/evals/data/tokenizers.lock.json`: tiktoken 0.12.0, both `cl100k_base` and `o200k_base`, exact locked wheels and BPE caches, `encode_ordinary`, UTF-8 with no normalization. Verify caches **before importing tokenizer code** and replace its cache reader with a hash-verified exact reader. Socket APIs are denied. A cache/wheel miss, corrupt file, changed requirement/encoding identity or unknown encoding is unmeasured, never fetched/replaced. Tokenize each delivered tool message separately, including its LF text-block separators, and sum counts; never concatenate messages to obtain merge savings. Requests, catalogs and complete wire results are likewise separate strings.

Reference mode requires the existing pinned Linux/amd64 CPython 3.13.7 image with `--network none`, read-only code/cache/wheel/input mounts and temporary offline hash-required installation in the container's `/tmp` only. It refuses a missing image. Exploratory mode uses an existing venv, records actual interpreter/module hashes and package versions, and closes no reference-tokenizer acceptance. Neither mode installs anything into the checkout or downloads anything. Cache location defaults are operational paths, not corpus-selection inputs.

The initial freeze binds the entire source/config/recipe/query/tokenizer contract and the generator inputs. The corpus freeze binds all 30 identities, inventories and task hashes. Development runs then record actual per-arm distributions and per-root source/delivered value byte contributions. `development-freeze.json` stores those observed distributions before heldout. Its prospective empirical byte/token ranges are the actual successful development min/max per era/arm, explicitly descriptive expectations rather than prediction intervals; empty development cells remain unmeasured. Reference token expectations remain unmeasured when only exploratory tokens exist. No simulated or guessed range is substituted. The exclusive `HELDOUT-UNSEALED.json` marker binds the corpus and development freezes before the first heldout task. Source/corpus/hash drift aborts. Every output directory is new; failures preserve a fixed-code private receipt, and there is no resume/retry overwrite path. Existing unsealed patients cannot be recycled as an independent holdout.

## Root run, after the official aggregate

Do not execute this driver while the aggregate owns the machine. It requires an explicit `--after-aggregate` operational acknowledgement and Node 24.22.0. Root first reviews this protocol, integrates the proposed guard entries, commits/freezes the source, and supplies any applicable official mutation evidence. No git write, full suite or mutation execution is performed by the driver.

```sh
export PATH="<node-24.22.0>/bin:$PATH"
export DOCKER_HOST=unix://<local-docker-socket>
export DOCKER_CONFIG="<docker-config-dir>"
node --import tsx scripts/s11/run.ts --after-aggregate --run s11-r1 --tokenizer-mode exploratory
```

Use `--tokenizer-mode reference` only when the pinned Python image already exists; missing means stop, not pull. Optional `--jar`, `--python`, `--cache` and `--wheelhouse` paths must be absolute. Optional `--mutation-shards <absolute-exact-source-14-shard-directory>` validates previously produced evidence only. No HAPI or Node image is started, no fixture edges are generated, and no unrelated container is inspected or stopped.

The driver executes: source/preregistration freeze; JAR/JDK/offline-tokenizer preflight and module inventory; serial twice-generated count-only screening; natural view transform and corpus/task freeze; 30 development instances × two eras × three formats; offline development tokenization and development freeze; exclusive heldout unseal; 24 primary heldout instances × two eras × three formats; offline tokenization; C.1–C.3 analysis with every attempt; final report/hash. Thus 180 development plus 144 heldout arm-era attempts = **324 attempted arm-era runs**, including unsupported entries. Raw captures, identities and eval details stay private; ordinary stdout contains only fixed statuses, counts and hashes.

At least 60 one-patient generator containers are needed, with more for excluded seeds; the bounded maximum is 1,536 containers (768 screened seeds × two). Generation/repeatability, natural stratum yield and task runtime are unmeasured here. Budget roughly **1–3 hours** for a well-populated run, with potentially many hours if pools are sparse; the per-generation existing timeout is ten minutes. These are planning estimates, not benchmark results. A missing input or depleted stratum stops before heldout. Root must publish the actual stage timings and unmet inputs beside any future result.

## Amendment 1, 2026-10-25 (before any S11 execution)

Status at amendment: the driver has never been executed. No S11 corpus has been generated, no `artifacts/m3b-measurement/` run or ledger exists, and the heldout cohort (seed pools 200000–200127, 201000–201127 and 202000–202127) has never been generated, opened, measured or unsealed. This amendment responds to an external review of the evaluation harness. It changes no corpus, seed pool, stratum, template, arm, era, operating point, tokenizer, metric, threshold, bootstrap or report rule: C.1, C.2 and C.3 above are unchanged. It changes how the driver protects the holdout and what the existing checks see.

1. **Single-use holdout across run names.** A persistent ledger, `artifacts/m3b-measurement/HELDOUT-LEDGER/`, sits beside every run directory (its upper-case name can never be a run name). Preflight refuses with `HELDOUT_ALREADY_CONSUMED`, before any generation, when any seed of the heldout pools was ever unsealed by any run. The unseal step first creates an exclusive (`wx`) `CONSUMED-<cohort key>.json` record there, then writes the run's `HELDOUT-UNSEALED.json`, which binds that record's hash. An append-only `events.jsonl` records every heldout custody (materialized) and unseal event; earlier exposures are copied into the next run's preregistration freeze. This is procedural custody on one operator's disk, not a security boundary: it cannot stop a person deleting the ledger.
2. **Custody separation before the development freeze.** Heldout raw exports, receipts and chart views are written under the run's separate `heldout-custody/` tree, never under the development-readable `screened/` and `charts/` trees, and the driver refuses to read a heldout chart (`HELDOUT_SEALED`) until the ledger consumption and the unseal marker exist. The preregistered seed ranges remain public and generation remains deterministic, so this separates custody; it does not make the heldout secret.
3. **A broken harness cannot consume the holdout.** Before unsealing, the driver refuses (`DEVELOPMENT_HARNESS_FAILURE`) when any development attempt carries a harness, confinement or upstream-count failure (`harness_error`, `upstream_confinement_missing`, `upstream_count_mismatch`) or a capture/accounting reason (`CATALOG_MISSING`, `WIRE_CAPTURE_MISSING`, `MODEL_CAPTURE_MISSING`, `EVAL_ACCOUNTING_MISMATCH`). Ordinary measured outcomes, including failed facts, are not grounds to stop.
4. **The required fidelity-mutant set.** The applicable set keeps its preregistered file rule. An applicable mutant declared in `scripts/mutation-survivors.json` is a reviewed equivalent mutant that the official aggregate rejects if killed, so it cannot be a required kill; it is frozen before generation as a declared survivor bound to its justification hash and must be reported as survived. Every other applicable mutant is a required kill. Supplied `--mutation-shards` evidence is validated at preflight, so invalid evidence stops the study before any chart exists, and again at analysis. A changed survivor set or mutant list is `FIDELITY_SET_CHANGED`. Missing evidence still leaves C.1 acceptance unmeasured.
5. **Existing checks applied at the delivered boundary.** The eval runner now trusts the configured natural patient (not the golden fixture patient) in its upstream confinement observer and metadata invariant; judges each S11 fact within its step's complete call chain with an explicit response index, so shorthand continuation pages resolve their query identity; requires the full-text-v1 delivered text to equal the raw `structuredContent` member, re-checks complete membership on the captured text, and scans the delivered text for prohibited markers; and applies the eval CLI's exact byte-accounting gate to every S11 attempt (`GATEWAY_ACCOUNTING_MISMATCH`). Ordinary eval output prints fixed causes, check ids and steps only.
